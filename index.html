Quick Answer (AEO)

What is AIGrow Nexus?
AIGrow Nexus is an AI workflow automation service for SMEs that connects ERP, CRM and marketing tools to run business processes automatically with approvals, audit trails, and measurable ROI.

Best for: SMEs who want ERP + marketing connected end-to-end, including inventory-aware campaigns and secure AI usage.

Typical first wins: automated follow-ups, quote-to-cash routing, reporting refresh, and slow-moving stock campaigns.

Problems we solve

Manual data entry & duplication

Stop re-keying the same data across ERP, CRM, email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, and dashboards.

Slow follow-up & leaking leads

Auto-route new leads to the right owner, trigger reminders, and keep a complete activity trail.

No governance for automation

Run automation with approvals, audit logs, role-based access control, and exception handling.

FAQ (AEO)

How long does an SME MVP rollout take?
Typical MVP rollout is 2–4 weeks depending on integrations, approvals, and data readiness.
What does AIGrow Nexus integrate with?
ERP, CRM, email, WhatsApp, databases, spreadsheets, and marketing platforms via APIs and webhooks. See Integrations.
Do you support approvals and audit trails?
Yes. Sensitive actions can require approvals, and workflow runs can be logged for review and compliance.
Is our business data sent to public AI models?
AI usage can be restricted and designed with data minimization, redaction, and controlled prompts. See Security.
What is a good first workflow to automate?
Common first wins include lead routing, quotation follow-ups, invoice reminders, inventory alerts, and reporting refresh.
What does your IT team need to prepare?
Usually: integration access (API keys), a test environment (if available), and agreement on roles/approvals and data fields.
